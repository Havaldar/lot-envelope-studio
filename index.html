Simplified model of NYC Zoning Resolution residential bulk rules. District values are approximate standard (non-bonus) figures, and many changed with the 2024 City of Yes amendments, so check each one against the current ZR text and ZoLa before relying on the output. Not modeled: open space ratio, towers, street wall location and recess rules, permitted obstructions, through lots, split lots, special districts, and the difference between gross and zoning floor area.
